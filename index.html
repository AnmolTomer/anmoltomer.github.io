How I work

Notes pinned to the map

  1. No. 1

    Start close to the problem.

    I work with sales, end customers and partner teams to understand requirements, turn them into technical solutions, and stay close through delivery.

  2. No. 2

    Move across the stack.

    Backend is home, but I enjoy the product and infrastructure conversations that make a build more complete.

  3. No. 3

    Keep learning where it matters.

    Right now, that means treating agentic AI less like a demo and more like a real workflow to design carefully.

Now

Currently shipping agentic AI workflows.

Models make decisions; the harness sets deterministic rules and checkpoints. I’m building around typed tools, evals, bounded retries, recovery and tracing, so failures can be understood and work can resume safely.

I’m drawn to the stretch between a good prototype and a product people can actually rely on.

Agent runfield log
  1. 1

    Plan

    Read the request, split it into steps, choose the tools each step needs.

  2. 2

    Call a tool

    Query the orders service through a typed FastAPI endpoint.

  3. ✕

    Tool times out

    The call fails after 8 seconds. Expected, and the plan allows for it.

  4. 4

    Recover

    Retry once with backoff, then fall back to the cached read from Redis.

  5. 5

    Trace it

    Every step, input and cost goes to Langfuse so a person can see why it answered this way.

  6. 6

    Answer

    Reply with the result, and say that the data came from the cache.
